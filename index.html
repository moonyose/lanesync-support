What LaneSync is

LaneSync times sprints with two iPhones, or with one iPhone and an Apple Watch, and needs no internet at the track. One device starts the run with a spoken “take your mark, set” and a starting gun; the finish iPhone films the line and detects each runner crossing it. You check each finish frame by frame, assign it to a runner, and save it. Every result is kept in History, with PB and SB badges.

Your first workout is free: one session on the finish iPhone, with as many runs as you like. Everything you save stays free to view, share as a result card, and delete.

LaneSync Pro is an optional subscription that adds unlimited workouts, CSV export of your times (and sharing sessions and personal bests as text), and analysis of the runs you record: progress charts per distance, personal-best progression, per-runner trends, and last run versus PB and recent average. Your saved results stay in History whether or not you subscribe.

Getting started

  1. Open LaneSync on both iPhones. On one choose START, on the other FINISH. (Using an Apple Watch as the starter? Choose FINISH on the iPhone, then Start from Apple Watch.)
  2. With the devices side by side, tap Sync clocks.
  3. Take the finish phone to the line, line up the guide on the finish line, and start detecting.
  4. On the start device, pick the distance and delay and tap START.
  5. When you're done, bring the devices back together to end the session, then review and save the times on the iPhone.

The phones can't find each other

  • Tap Retry in the connection bar.
  • Check that Local Network is on for LaneSync on both phones: Settings › LaneSync › Local Network.
  • Turn on both Bluetooth and Wi-Fi in Control Center on both phones. You don't need to join a network.
  • Keep the phones within a few metres of each other while they connect.

Apple Watch tips

  • Unlock your iPhone at least once after it restarts, and keep LaneSync open on both the iPhone and the watch while syncing.
  • Allow the Health (workout) permission on the watch. LaneSync uses a workout session only to stay awake during the countdown; it reads no Health data and saves no workout.

“Free workout used”

Each iPhone can time one workout without LaneSync Pro. It counts once the finish iPhone has sent its times to the start phone, or saved them in an Apple Watch session; a session you discard, or one with no finish in it, doesn't count. After that, choosing FINISH opens the LaneSync Pro plans. START is never limited, and History stays free to view.

Restore purchases

  1. Open LaneSync and tap the LaneSync Pro row on the first screen (or tap ⋯ › LaneSync Pro…).
  2. Tap Restore Purchases. You may be asked to sign in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

A subscription works on every iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID.

Manage or cancel your subscription

  • In LaneSync: open the LaneSync Pro screen and tap Manage Subscription (shown while you are subscribed).
  • Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › LaneSync Pro.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. After cancelling, Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. Afterwards all of your saved results stay in History, and a session you've already started can always be finished and saved; starting new workouts, CSV export and the progress charts need Pro again.

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
